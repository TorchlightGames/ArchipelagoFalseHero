## What is False Hero?

False Hero is a fast-paced soulslike adventure where you steal your enemies’ attacks and chain them into deadly combos.
Explore the corrupted Land of the Gods and put your combat skills to the true test in challenging boss fights.
Will you fight for the Gods or become an apostle of Death?

## What does randomization do?

All item pickups are randomized. This includes inks, consumables, scrolls, clothing and key items.

All Insight drops are randomized. Insight can be picked up in any order. Enemies will drop the same amount of Insight as in the base game. (For example Knowledge Keepers drop 3 Insight, while bosses drop only 1)
The amount of required Insight to learn Combat Arts can be customized.

The gods powers (excluding the Blade of Wisdom) are randomized. This includes Vines of Birth, Wings of Hope and the Red Flame of Passion.

The 4 essences of the gods are randomized. These are required to pass through the Death-tentacle doors.

Missable checks like the Physician of Birth questline will never include progression items.

## What's the goal?

The goal is either to find the 4 essences of the gods and defeat the Deathborn, or to find and defeat all 13 bosses.
