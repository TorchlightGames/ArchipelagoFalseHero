## Location names

All locations begin with an abbreviation indicating their general region.

* MTW: Mountaintops of Wisdom
* EMT: Edge of the Mountaintops
* PD: Prison of Death
* LS: Lower Swamp
* FP: Fallen Paradise
* BSM: Base of the Split Mountain
* TB: Temple of Birth
* TBO: Temple of Birth Outside
* BT: Border Tower
* DV: Decrepit Village
* BHT: Base of Hopes Temple
* TH: Temple of Hope
* HV: Hidden Village
* WTC: Wartorn Canyon
* EBF: Eternal Battlefield
* RRF: Root of the Red Flame
* RD: Root of Death
